What the site offers, how to pick the right tool, what happens to the text you enter, and where the limits are.

Overview

Kromo Tools is a free website of browser-based utilities: calculators, converters, PDF and image tools, health estimators, time helpers and social media generators. No account or signup is required. The site is run by founder Dawood Mustafa with a small team, and is paid for by advertising and optional support.

Most tools run entirely inside your browser. A smaller group, the AI tools, send your text to a backend and then to an outside AI provider. The difference matters for privacy, so it has its own section below.

Quick start

  1. Open the All Tools page and type a keyword such as pdf, bmi or age, or use the category buttons.
  2. Open the tool page. Each one explains how it works and which formula it is based on.
  3. Enter your values or choose a file. Do not enter passwords, bank details or medical records.
  4. Read the result and treat it as an estimate. For anything important, check it independently.
  5. If a result looks wrong, use Report a Bug. If a tool is missing, use Request a Tool.

    How the tools work

    Browser-based tools

    Calculators, converters and most text, image and PDF tools run on your own device. According to the site, the content is not sent to its servers, and it disappears when you close or refresh the page.

    AI-powered tools

    Tools such as the AI Text Humanizer, AI Chatbot Builder or Grammar Checker may use an AI service. The site says your text goes to its Vercel-hosted backend and on to an outside AI provider, and that it does not store the text itself. Vercel and the provider handle it under their own policies.

    Usage limits

    Some AI tools are rate-limited. To do this, the backend reads your IP address and keeps a short-lived counter with Upstash. The terms forbid bypassing limits, for example by rotating IP addresses or using automated requests.

    Limits and accuracy

    • Estimates only. Health, nutrition and finance tools give general estimates. They are not medical, legal, tax or financial advice.
    • How formulas are checked. The site says it uses published sources such as WHO guidance, tests against manual calculations, and re-checks each tool every 90 days.
    • AI output. It can be wrong, outdated or similar to other users' results. Edit before use and follow school, employer or platform rules about AI-assisted work.
    • Rounding and standards. Different countries and formulas can give slightly different answers.
    • Availability. Tools are "as is" and may change, pause or be removed.

    Troubleshooting

    A tool does not load or the button does nothing

    Refresh the page, clear your cache, and try another browser. Disable extensions that block scripts for this site.

    An AI tool says the limit is reached

    Fair-use limits apply per IP address and time period. Wait and try again later. The limits can change at any time.

    A calculator result looks wrong

    Check units and rounding first. If it still looks wrong, send the tool name, your inputs, the result and the result you expected through Report a Bug. A screenshot helps, but hide personal details.

    A large PDF or image is slow

    Browser tools use your device's memory. Close other tabs, or split the file first.

    FAQ

    Is Kromo Tools really free?

    Yes. The site says all tools are free with no account. Optional support and advertising pay for hosting.

    Can I trust the health calculators?

    Use them for general guidance only. They cannot diagnose anything and do not know your medical history.

    Do AI tools keep my text?

    The site says it does not store it, but the backend and AI provider process it under their own policies.

    Can I get a tool built for me?

    Yes, through the Custom Tool Development page. You get a quote before anything is built.

    Is this guide official?

    Yes. It is published by the Kromo Tools team. The tools themselves live at kromotools.net.
